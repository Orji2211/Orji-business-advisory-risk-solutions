About the Founder

Paul C. CRISC, PMP

Founder & Principal Consultant

Paul C. is the Founder and Principal Consultant of Orji Business Advisory & Risk Solutions, a consulting firm focused on helping organizations improve operations, strengthen governance, manage risk, and drive sustainable business performance.

Paul brings experience spanning risk management, issue management, governance, audit support, project management, and business process improvement. Throughout his career, he has supported teams and business functions within leading financial institutions, including Citibank, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, and Capital One.

Professional Credentials

  • Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC)
  • Project Management Professional (PMP)

To help organizations identify opportunities, solve business challenges, strengthen governance, manage risk, and achieve measurable results through practical advisory solutions.
